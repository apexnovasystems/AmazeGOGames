Privacy Policy for PaintRoll

Last updated: July 11, 2026

ApexNova Systems ("we", "us") built PaintRoll, a puzzle game for Android. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Information we collect

PaintRoll does not require an account, login, or any personal information to play. Your game progress — levels completed, coins, stars, achievements, and settings — is stored only on your own device and is never transmitted to us or to any server we control.

Advertising

PaintRoll shows ads (banner, interstitial, and rewarded video) through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • App activity related to ad interactions (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly. You can review and control how Google uses this data, including opting out of personalized ads, at adssettings.google.com. For further detail, see Google's own policies below.

In-app purchases

PaintRoll offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs, Remove Ads) processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment information — that is handled directly by Google.

Children's privacy

PaintRoll is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

Data retention and your choices

Since gameplay data lives only on your device, uninstalling PaintRoll removes it entirely. Advertising identifiers collected by Google are subject to Google's own retention and deletion policies — see the link above to manage or reset your advertising ID.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
